Biography

As a child, Ingeborg was always the creative one. She spent most of her free time drawing, crafting 3D birthday cards, and experimenting with anything that allowed her to make something from scratch.

What she struggled with was finding a larger outlet for that creativity. At twelve, she began looking up to bloggers and even tried starting one herself. It failed quickly, but it opened the door to something more interesting. Designing blog headers became a new creative obsession, and she taught herself Photoshop to bring her ideas to life.

What began as a hobby quickly expanded into learning HTML and CSS, allowing her to build her own blog and customize every color and layout exactly as she envisioned.

She knew from that moment that she enjoyed making things for people. Back then, she imagined becoming a fashion designer or architect. At the time, she didn't realize that her interests were already pointing her toward what would later become her career → UX design.

Already as a kid, her mom had already purchased ingeborg.no in the hopes of her using it someday to showcase her work (or that a princess would be named Ingeborg and purchase the domain for a lot of money).

Unfortunately, neither happened. Instead, Ingeborg now use Figma as her creative stage, sharing designs that live far beyond a single domain name.
